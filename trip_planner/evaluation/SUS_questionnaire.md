# Usability questionnaire: Lanka Trip Planner

**Task (before the questionnaire):** "Using the app, plan a trip that fits you: choose a region, your interests,
trip details and travel style, then open your itinerary, places and budget." The researcher records whether the
task was completed without help and the time from tapping *Start planning* to seeing the itinerary.

**Participant group:** ☐ International visitor  ☐ Sri Lankan resident
